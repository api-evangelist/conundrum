# Conundrum

> Conundrum develops AI-driven, closed-loop advanced process control software for metals, mining, and cement plants.

## Primary pages

- [Conundrum platform](https://conundrum.ai/): Product overview, deployment options, supported processes, and measured operational footprint.
- [Industrial AI case studies](https://conundrum.ai/case-studies/): Index of published process-control results.
- [Flotation — reducing reagent dosage](https://conundrum.ai/case-studies/flotation-reducing-reagent-dosage): Mean reagent feed rate reduced from 20.22 to 19.67 grams per ton of ore.
- [Stabilizing SAG mill energy consumption](https://conundrum.ai/case-studies/stabilizing-energy-consumption-of-the-sag-mill): Power standard deviation reduced from 246 kW to 128 kW.

## Product scope

Conundrum applies adaptive process models and real-time control to crushing, grinding, flotation, and cement production. The platform supports on-premises and cloud deployment. Refer to the linked pages for the complete context and limitations of each published result.
